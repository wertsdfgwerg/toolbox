# 🤖 AI Toolbox

**Free, simple and practical AI productivity tools.**

AI Toolbox is a collection of free online AI tools for writing, studying, job applications, and everyday productivity.

🌐 **Website:** https://wertsdfgwerg.github.io/toolbox/

## ✨ Features

### ✍️ Writing & Productivity

* **AI Prompt Generator** — Generate useful prompts for different tasks.
* **AI Article Summarizer** — Quickly summarize long text.
* **AI Writing Polisher** — Make writing clearer, smoother, and more natural.
* **AI Email Assistant** — Create and improve emails.
* **AI Reply Assistant** — Generate natural replies for different situations.

### 📱 Content Creation

* **AI Title Generator** — Generate titles for different types of content.
* **Xiaohongshu Title Generator** — Generate titles for Xiaohongshu posts.

### 📄 Career

* **AI Resume Assistant** — Improve and organize resume content.
